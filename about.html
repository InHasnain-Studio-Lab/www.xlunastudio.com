About

A studio named after a new moon

A new moon is the start of every cycle: dark at first, then brighter each night. XLUNA begins the same way, with a small catalogue that grows one careful release at a time.

The name

X marks the orbit. Luna is the moon.

The mark is a new moon with two orbits crossing in front of it. Together they draw the X. Each orbit stands for an app moving around the studio, and the moon is the promise at the centre: you own what you buy.

XLUNA MARK · NEW MOON, TWO ORBITS
Founder

Hasnain Butt Akhtar

XLUNA is an independent studio, designed and developed by Hasnain Butt Akhtar. Every app is planned, built, tested and shipped in-house, with no investors steering it towards subscriptions or data collection.

How we build

Four rules for every app

PRIVATE

No telemetry, no accounts. Your work stays on your device.

HONEST

A real trial, a clear price, and no dark patterns at checkout.

CRAFTED

Fewer apps, finished properly, rather than many half-built ones.
