Every lunar cycle starts in darkness and ends in full light. XLUNA starts the same way: a small studio, a short list of apps, and a sky that fills in one careful release at a time.

The mark

X is a blade of light crossing the moon. Luna is the moon itself.

The mark is a new moon's first crescent, cut through by a single beam of light. Together they draw the X: the moon is the promise that what you buy stays yours, and the beam is every app that sets out from it.
