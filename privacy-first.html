The promise

Five things every XLUNA™ app keeps to

01

Your PC does the work

Wallpapers are drawn, sound is enhanced and your desk is built on your own machine. Nothing is sent away to be processed.

02

No account

No sign-up, no sign-in, no email address. Install from the Microsoft Store and start.

03

No telemetry, no adverts

No analytics, no tracking and no advertising code in any XLUNA app. There is nothing to opt out of, because nothing is collected.

04

One payment

Try every feature free for 24 hours, then buy once and the licence is yours. Nothing renews.

05

Leaves cleanly

Each app keeps its settings in its own folder, and uninstalling removes it. Files you saved to your own folders stay yours.

Buying and trials

The Microsoft Store handles payment

Trials and purchases go through the Microsoft Store. Microsoft takes the payment, and XLUNA never sees or stores your card details or your Microsoft account information.

Each app checks its licence with the Microsoft Store to confirm your trial or purchase. That check is handled by Microsoft and carries nothing from XLUNA.
