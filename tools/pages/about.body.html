The inception

Every full moon begins as a thin crescent

XLUNA did not start with investors, a boardroom or a big team. It started with one developer, one belief and the patience to build, release after release.

  1. Chapter 1 · The spark

    A simple belief

    Hasnain Butt Akhtar believed something the software industry had started to forget: when you pay for a tool, it should be yours. Not rented by the month, not locked behind an account, not watching what you do. That belief was the spark for everything that followed.

  2. Chapter 2 · Proof

    Hasnain Studio X

    The belief became a studio. Hasnain Studio X grew into a catalogue of 79 local-first apps for Windows and Android, all designed, coded and shipped by its founder. It proved that one independent maker, working with care, can build what people usually expect from a whole company.

  3. Chapter 3 · The lesson

    Ownership is the feature

    Every app taught the same lesson. People want tools they can trust: no subscriptions, no sign-ups, and AI that runs on their own machine with their data kept close. Those weren't features to add later. They were the whole point.

  4. Chapter 4 · The new moon

    XLUNA is born

    In 2026 that lesson got a home of its own. XLUNA is named for the new moon, the quiet moment when a cycle begins and the sky is still dark. Not an ending, a beginning: a studio built from its first line around the promise that what you buy stays yours.

  5. Chapter 5 · The light ahead

    Building for the next ten years

    The first XLUNA apps are being built now across seven orbits. They are made to still be working, and still be yours, long after today's trends have faded. Each release adds a little more light, the way a crescent grows night by night into a full moon.

"The sky is darkest at the new moon. That is exactly when the light begins."XLUNA Studio
The concept

Everything in XLUNA comes from the night sky

The name

X is the crossing point. Luna is the moon. Together they name a studio built around one fixed promise, with apps travelling around it.

The mark

A new moon's first crescent, cut by a beam of light. The crescent is the start of every cycle; the beam is each app setting out from it. Together they draw the X.

The phases

The studio's roadmap follows the lunar cycle, from new moon to full. The site even shows tonight's real phase, calculated live in your browser.

The orbits

Apps are grouped into seven orbits by what they help you do: Create, Think, Power, Connect, Play, Build and Explore.

Built to last

Designed to keep working, whatever comes next

An app you buy should outlive trends, price changes and even the studio's own plans. XLUNA designs for that from the start.

LASTING 01

No server to switch off

Apps run on your device, so they do not stop working if a cloud service changes or closes.

LASTING 02

No account to lose

There is no login to forget, lock or expire. The licence comes from the store you bought it from.

LASTING 03

Your files stay readable

Work is saved in common, open formats wherever possible, so it can move with you to other tools.

LASTING 04

AI that grows with your hardware

On-device models get faster as your computer improves, with no per-use fees and no data sent away.

Studio code

Four rules for every app

CODE 01

Owned

Pay once and the app keeps working, keeps improving and stays on your machine.

CODE 02

Private

No telemetry and no accounts. What you make is nobody's business but yours.

CODE 03

One clear price, a real trial and a checkout without tricks.

CODE 04

Fewer apps, each completed properly, instead of a crowd of half-built ones.
